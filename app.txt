App Name: Contractor Miles
Version: 1.0.0
Platform: Android / iOS
Category: Tax / Mileage Tracking
Target Market: Global
Price Model: Free 40 trips/month + $4.99/month Pro

Description:
Uses background location to auto-detect business trips and prompts contractors with a single-swipe interface to log or discard each trip for IRS-compliant mileage deductions.

Key Features:
- Background trip detection using geofencing and motion sensors
- One-swipe confirm business trip or discard personal trip
- IRS Standard Mileage Rate auto-applied (2025: $0.70/mile)
- Manual trip entry as fallback
- Purpose categories: Client Visit, Supply Run, Office, Medical, Charity
- Trip history with map preview of each route
- Monthly/annual deduction summary report
- PDF report export for tax filing or accountant
- Round-trip calculator for known regular routes
- Vehicle profile: enter car and switch between multiple vehicles

Target Audience: Freelancers, real estate agents, consultants, home service contractors
Competitors: MileIQ, Everlance, TripLog
Unique Value: One-swipe confirm UX is the fastest mileage logging experience available
